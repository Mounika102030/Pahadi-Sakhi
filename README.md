# 🌄 Pahadi Sakhi

### *From the hills to your home*

---

## 📌 Overview

**Pahadi Sakhi** is a voice-enabled e-commerce platform designed to empower rural women artisans from hilly regions by providing them direct access to digital marketplaces.

The platform focuses on **accessibility, sustainability, and storytelling**, enabling women with low digital literacy to manage products using simple voice interactions.

---

## 🚨 Problem

Rural women artisans face:

* Low digital literacy
* Lack of direct market access
* Dependency on middlemen
* Geographic isolation

As a result, they earn significantly less and remain disconnected from larger markets.

---

## 💡 Solution

Pahadi Sakhi provides:

* 🎤 Voice-enabled interaction for ease of use
* 🛒 Direct-to-customer marketplace
* 📖 Story-based product representation
* 🌱 Focus on eco-friendly handmade products

---

## ✨ Features

### 👩‍🌾 Seller Side

* Add products easily
* Voice command support (prototype)
* Simple dashboard for low-complexity usage

### 🛒 Customer Side

* Browse handmade products
* View artisan profiles
* Voice search (basic implementation)

### 🌐 Platform

* Role-based login (Seller / Customer)
* Product management system
* Backend API with database integration
